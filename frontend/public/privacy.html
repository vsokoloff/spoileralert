Spoiler Alert — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Spoiler Alert ("the app", "we", "us") helps you track food in your fridge, reduce waste, and get recipe ideas. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the app:

  • Account details: your name and email address. Your password is stored only as a securely hashed value — we never store or see your actual password.
  • Your food data: the items you add (names, categories, storage locations, purchase and expiration dates, quantities), and any notes or roommate tags you create.
  • Receipt images: if you use receipt scanning, the photo you provide is sent for processing to extract food items (see "Third-party services" below). We do not retain the receipt image after extraction.
  • Chat messages: messages you send to the SPOY recipe assistant, so it can respond and show your history.

We do not collect location data, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising. We do not use tracking or serve ads.

How we use your information

  • To provide core features: storing your inventory, tracking expiration, sending in-app notifications, and generating recipe suggestions.
  • To authenticate you and keep your data private to your account.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Third-party services

To power receipt scanning and the SPOY recipe assistant, relevant content (such as a receipt image or your current fridge contents) is processed by Google's Gemini API. That data is sent only to generate the requested result and is handled under Google's terms. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data storage

Your data is stored in a secure cloud database and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: open Notifications & settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data (food items, notifications, chat history) and cannot be undone.

Children

Spoiler Alert is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
