## What we collect

- **Account information**: your name and email address, used to create and
  identify your account.
- **Food inventory data**: the items you add, their categories, locations,
  expiration dates, and consumption/deletion history.
- **Receipt images**: photos of grocery receipts you choose to scan. Images
  are sent to Google's Gemini API to extract food items and are **not stored
  on our servers** after processing.
- **Chat messages**: messages you send to the SPOY assistant, and the
  assistant's replies, stored to maintain your conversation history.
- **Roommate connections**: links between your account and roommates you add
  by email.

## What we do NOT collect

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or
analytics/tracking data. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Third-party services

- **Google Gemini API** (Google LLC): receipt images and SPOY chat messages
  (including a summary of your current food inventory) are processed by
  Google's Gemini API to provide scanning and assistant features. See
  [Google's API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) for how Google
  handles this data.
- **[HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g., Render/Railway]**: our backend and database are
  hosted on [PROVIDER].

## Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can permanently
delete your account and all associated data (items, notifications, chat
history, roommate links) at any time from the app's settings, or by
contacting us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## Security

Passwords are stored hashed (bcrypt); all traffic uses HTTPS; access to your
data requires an authenticated session token.

## Children

Spoiler Alert is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them.
